Questions

Sec. 06

Straight answers.

Q1 How long does installation take? +

Most residential garages are completed in a single day. Your written job instructions state when walking, normal use, and vehicle traffic may resume — timing depends on the installed system, slab repairs, temperature, and humidity.

Q2 Is this the same as epoxy? +

No — and the difference matters in Redding. Traditional garage-floor epoxy can be more rigid, may discolor with UV exposure, and is more vulnerable to hot-tire pickup. Our polyurea base coat is engineered to be more flexible and more abrasion-resistant than traditional epoxy, and the polyaspartic top coat is formulated to resist yellowing and fading under intense sun.

Q3 What about my cracked or pitted concrete? +

Repair is built into our process. We grind, fill, and profile damage before any coating goes down, so the finished floor is smooth and sound.

Q4 What does the warranty cover? +

Residential installations carry a written limited warranty covering both product and workmanship. We'll walk you through the exact terms in writing before any work begins.
